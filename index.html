THE FOUR PILLARS

Scholarship

A commitment to learning and striving for academic excellence, recognizing that education is the foundation of a successful future.

Service

The voluntary contribution made by a student to the school or community, done without compensation and with a positive, courteous, and enthusiastic spirit.

Leadership

Taking the initiative in class and school activities, demonstrating reliability, and inspiring positive behavior in others.

Character

Maintaining high standards of honesty and reliability, showing courtesy, concern, and respect for others at all times.
